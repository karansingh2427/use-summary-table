          "Replanted" — the agent did not re-scan for additional application-direction
          sections after the main section loop.
CORRECT:  11 separate rows for: Blueberry Highbush, Caneberry 13-07A, Coffee,

          separate use patterns and require their own rows. → R-17

=== 2026-08-07 · Grapes 13-07F · Max Total Rate/Yr · p. 15 ===
MISTAKE:  Max Total Rate/Yr recorded as "0.065 (<1% OM); 0.13 (≥1% OM)".
          This doubles the label's stated annual cap for ≥1% OM soils.
CAUSE:    Agent applied R-4 OM-split logic incorrectly. The label gives one rate
          for both OM bands for grapes: max 5.0 fl oz/A (0.065 lb ai/A) per year,
          regardless of OM. No ≥1% OM variant exists on this label.
CORRECT:  Page 15: "Do not apply more than a total of 5.0 fl oz product/A per year."
          Max Total Rate/Yr = 0.065 (single value, no OM split).

          per OM bracket. Check each crop's own rate table — do not infer an OM split
          from other crops in the same label. → R-4
